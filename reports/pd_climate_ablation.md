# Climate ablation of the PD model

Generated by `python -m climrisk.models.ablation`. Same models, fitted on the training vintages without climate features (base) and with each climate feature set; evaluated on the validation vintages. Gains with a 95% paired bootstrap interval (200 resamples of individual loans: ZIP3 dependence, model fitting and validation-based early stopping are not included). Test vintages not used.

Feature sets: `climate_all` = exposure_main, exposure_broad, recent_events_12m, near_coast_share, log_dist_coast; `climate_static` = near_coast_share, log_dist_coast.
