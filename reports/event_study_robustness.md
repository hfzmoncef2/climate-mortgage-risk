## 3. What happens after a default?

Loans labelled default by D004 (within the first 24 months from first payment), whose first qualifying default occurred from 2014-01-01, outside COVID, followed for 24 months. Flags are taken in the default month or the 2 months before. 'loss' = loss exit or REO (not necessarily a realized accounting loss); exits use zero_balance_date with reporting month as fallback. Follow-up runs through default date +24 months. 'reperforming' = ever back to current or prepaid, provided no loss was observed. 'current_at_end_pct' uses all defaults as its denominator and requires an observed current status exactly at +24 months, with no loss/REO, prepayment or transfer in follow-up. It is a separate measure, not another mutually exclusive outcome. Missing endpoint records and paid-off loans do not count as current. Defaults during COVID are excluded; follow-up windows may overlap COVID. This is not a lifetime-default cohort. Missing terminal follow-up is unknown, not evidence of continuing delinquency. Loss takes precedence over any cure; these are descriptive outcomes, not causal comparisons of assistance programs.
