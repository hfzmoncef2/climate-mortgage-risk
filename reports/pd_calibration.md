## Level and slope

Perfect calibration: observed / predicted = 1, a = 0, b = 1. a is the shift of logit(PD) that makes the mean PD equal the observed rate (a < 0: PDs too high). b < 1: PDs too extreme; b > 1: PDs too flat.
