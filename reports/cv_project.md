# CV project entry

**Climate Risk in Mortgage Credit** *(in progress)* — Python, DuckDB, SQL, pytest

- Built a tested, reproducible pipeline on 1.05M Freddie Mac loans (62M loan-months), FEMA disaster declarations and Census housing data.
- Designed a 24-month default target with censoring, selection-bias and data-gap controls, documented in a decision log.
- Raw evidence: Houston-area 60+ day delinquency rose from 0.7% to 3.9% within three months of Hurricane Harvey, vs. 1.1% to 1.5% in the Texas comparison group; event study in progress.
