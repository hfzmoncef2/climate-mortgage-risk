# Loss given default (LGD) of D004 defaults

Generated by `python -m climrisk.risk.lgd`. D004 defaults of the PD sample with a complete 60-month follow-up after the default month. EAD = balance at default. Loss = Freddie Mac actual loss for loss exits (the period's severity when the amount is not published), 0 for payoffs and loans current at the end of the follow-up. Transfers, loans still delinquent and loans whose records stop before the end of the follow-up are unresolved: their loss is unknown.

Columns: `resolved_pct` share of defaults with a known (or imputed) loss; `loss_exit_pct` share of resolved defaults ending in a loss exit; `severity_pct` loss / EAD of the reported loss exits; `lgd_pct` loss / EAD over resolved defaults; `lgd_low_pct` and `lgd_high_pct` bound it over all defaults, counting every unresolved default as no loss (low) or as a loss exit at the period's severity (high); `mod_cost_pct` cumulative modification costs / EAD, reported separately.
